ShePrints3D exists to make custom product development more approachable. You don’t need a finished model, a perfect sketch or experience with CAD to start.

Maybe your electronics need an enclosure that actually fits. Maybe your project is missing one very specific part. Or maybe you want an everyday object that feels like it was made for you.

We combine creative problem solving, 3D design and additive manufacturing to help move your idea from a conversation to a physical product — with room to test, learn and refine along the way.
